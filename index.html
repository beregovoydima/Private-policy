🔒 Introduction

Paper Notes ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

All essential note-taking features will remain completely free forever:

  • ✅ Unlimited notes, lists, and calendar events

  • ✅ Local notifications and reminders
  • ✅ No ads, ever
⭐ We do NOT collect any personal data.

📊 Data Collection

Paper Notes is designed with privacy as a core principle. We believe your data belongs to you, and only you.

Current App:

  • ✅ No user accounts required
  • ✅ No registration or login
  • ✅ No personal information collected
  • ✅ No analytics or tracking
  • ✅ No third-party services
  • ✅ No advertising networks
  • ✅ All data stored locally on your device only

💾 Data Storage

All your notes, lists, and calendar events are stored locally on your device only:

  • ✅ Saved in your device's local storage (SQLite database)
  • ✅ No data transmitted to external servers
  • ✅ No data shared with third parties
  • ✅ You have complete control over your data

🔑 Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

📱 Notifications Permission (Optional)

Used to send you reminders for calendar events and tasks. Can be disabled at any time in your device settings.

💾 Storage Permission

Used to save your notes, lists, and events on your device's local storage. No data is uploaded or transmitted.

Note: All permissions are used solely for app functionality and do not involve any data collection or transmission to external servers.

🚫 Third-Party Services

Paper Notes does NOT currently use any third-party services:

No Google Analytics No Firebase No Facebook SDK No Ad Networks No Social Media APIs No Cloud Services No Tracking Tools

🛡️ Data Security

Your data is as secure as your device:

  • All data stays on your device
  • No transmission over the internet
  • No risk of server breaches or data leaks
  • Your device's built-in security (encryption, lock screen) protects your notes
  • Only you have access to your data

👶 Children's Privacy

Paper Notes does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. The app is safe for all ages. No data is collected, making it compliant with COPPA and similar regulations.

🔄 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top
  • Showing a notice in the app for significant changes
  • Requesting new consent if we start collecting data

⚖️ Your Rights

Since we don't collect any data:

  • Data Access: All your data is already on your device
  • Data Control: You have complete control over your local data
  • Data Deletion: Clear data in app settings or uninstall the app
  • No Tracking: We don't track your usage or behavior

🗑️ Data Deletion

To delete your data:

  1. Open the app → Settings → Clear Data, or
  2. Uninstall the app from your device

This will permanently delete all local notes, lists, and events. There is no backup on our servers.
