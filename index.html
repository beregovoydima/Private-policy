Required for authentication and cloud storage. The app requests access to:

Scope: The app only requests access to files it creates in your Google Drive. It does not access other files in your Drive.

📱 Notifications Permission (Optional)

Used to send you reminders for calendar events and tasks. Can be disabled at any time in your device settings.

💾 Storage Permission

Used for local caching and temporary storage on your device. Your primary data is stored in Google Drive.

Note: All permissions are used solely for app functionality. We do not use permissions to collect data for analytics or advertising.

All data is stored in the user's Google Drive account, which is subject to Google's privacy policies and age restrictions.

⚖️ Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Data Access: All your data is stored in your Google Drive account, accessible through Google Drive or the app
  • Data Control: You have complete control over your data through your Google account settings
  • Data Portability: You can export your data from Google Drive at any time
  • Data Deletion: You can delete your data from Google Drive or revoke app access
  • No Tracking: We don't track your usage or behavior
  • Account Revocation: You can revoke app access to your Google account at any time

🗑️ Data Deletion

To delete your data, you have several options:

  1. Delete from Google Drive: Access your Google Drive and delete the files created by Paper Notes
  2. Revoke App Access: Go to your Google Account settings → Security → Third-party apps → Revoke access for Paper Notes
  3. Uninstall the App: Uninstalling the app will remove local cached data, but your data in Google Drive will remain unless you delete it

Note: Since your data is stored in your Google Drive account, deleting the app does not automatically delete your data from Google Drive. You must manually delete files from Google Drive if you want to remove them permanently.
